Questions

Things people usually ask.

Something else? Ask us directly

Do I need the internet to use your software?

No. Our software runs on your own computer and keeps working when the internet doesn’t: billing, printing, reports and backups. Your data stays with you.

Can you build something just for my business?

Yes, that’s what we do. Tell us how your business works and we’ll design the software around it, show you the screens first and send a written quote.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the product and the customisation you need. We always send a written quote first. Licences are yearly, and the first year of updates is free.

Will you set it up for us?

Yes. We install it, connect your printers and scanners, move your data from your old software and train your staff.

What if something goes wrong?

Message us on WhatsApp or call. You talk to the engineers who built your software, and we can help remotely or at your shop.
